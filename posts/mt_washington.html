Mt Washington Hillclimb - Hadrian Ward

What is it

Every year in August there is a bike race up the Mt Washington auto road. It is 7.6 miles and 4,690 feet of elevation gain. Once at the top everyone must wait for the race to finish to drive back down. Every rider must ride down in a car because riding down would overheat a bikes brakes. People would probably go too fast anyway and fly off the corners.

My attempts

I first heard about it in 2023 from my neighbor Sid, who had done it ages ago. In 2024 I signed up and raced the first time getting a time of 1:17:13. It was an insane experience, weirdly calm compared to other bike races because everyone is going so slow. In normal bike races people clump up to draft off eachother which is more efficient. In this race there is no point in drafting which made it more of an individual event. In 2025 I finished in 1:21:09 probably from not eating enough and going to fast in the beginning.

Bike setup

Normal road gears aren't low enough so every year I put mountain bike gears on the road bike. In 2026 I am even ditching the 2nd front sprocket to make it a 1-by.
