Bike KOM Tach - Hadrian Ward

What is it?

In 2026 I wanted to beat my fastest time up Mt Washington which I set in 2024. I downloaded the strava data from the 2024 race and used a Python program to create a series of (time, distance) points. I then had an Arduino compare the record-run points to what it measured from the wheel rotations in real time and give me a time gap.

Did it work?

Yes. About halfway into the 2026 race I started to fall behind and started doubting that it was accurate (of course I was just being stupid and slow). I missid my PR by 4 secs which is consistent with it showing 15+ secs behind right after I had finished.

Parts

  • Arduino
  • LEDs x 5
  • 9V battery
  • Battery to barrel jack connector
  • 2x Wires
  • Metal tape, plastic bits to connect to electrodes on bike
  • Bike magnetic reed switch tachometer (already on bike)

User interface

There will be 4 LEDs counting 30-second intervals in Binary the time ahead or behind of the record time, rounding down. A 5th LED will be ON if ahead, OFF if behind. I hope I can keep that LED ON the whole time.

To start: Press the arduino reset button, the timer will start when 5 tach pulses have already been detected. At the start line make sure that the magnet is not near the switch so as not to cause it to switch repeatedly with small movements.
