Tower & Anemometer - Hadrian Ward

Origin

Way back, me (and somewhat my brother) had an idea to build something called The Structure. Mom suggested using compressed-earth-bricks. Instead I ended up building a square tower from 2" X 2" wood pieces leftover from something.

Improvements, Setbacks

It reached 3 stories and at some point had a swing and an elevator with a box and a winch to haul tools up to the top. I don't remember how many times but it got blown down many times. I remember always looking out the window after a big storm to check that it was still up. At some point I put 4 ropes in it to hold it up, although the wind still blew the stakes out multiple times. I then put heavy rocks in the bottom on the "ground floor" which also didn't work. I remember going out in the wind and rain to move the heaviest rocks to whatever side the wind was comming from.

Anemometer

At some point I decided to make a windmill style anemometer to put on the tower. Just like the tower itself the anemometer went through many iterations to survive the wind.
