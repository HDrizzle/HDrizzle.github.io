Working at Second Story Builders, LLC - Hadrian Ward

Deconstruction

Photo credits: Becca Troast

Most of the my work was in deconstruction meaning taking apart houses and deciding what material is worth saving. Over the summer there were two deconstruction sites, both in Rockland, ME.

Reuse

Photo credits: Becca Troast, Danny Salomon, Danny Salomon

The company sells a lot of salvaged material and also uses it for new construction. We were modifying another structure at one of the deconstruction sites in Rockland using salvaged wood. When I was there we were finishing a storage shed in St George. I helped put together, varnish, and mount the doors and finish the ramp for that shed.
