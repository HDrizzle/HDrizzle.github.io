Capacitor Bank Coil Gun - Hadrian Ward

Many years ago I was given some junk which included 6 x 2,200 uF 450 V capacitors. Summer of 2025 I was bored and watched railgun videos on youtube. A railgun needs to be strong and it wears away with every use so I decided a coilgun would be better. I started by making a coil of thick wire and placing a metal marble in it. When I shorted the capacitors with it, it flung the marble out of the coil. I improved it by adding a wood structure around the capacitors and a crude switch. As seen in the photos the switch probably bounces due to both magnetic forces and plasma. A future improvement would be to use a V-shaped switch which falls into a socket, inspired by A video suggesting an improved high-current switch for Styropyro's 400 car batteries.
